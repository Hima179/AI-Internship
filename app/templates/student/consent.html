Informed Consent

Please read the following information carefully

1. Purpose of the Assessment

The primary purpose of this psychological assessment is to provide insights into your mental well-being and personality traits. This data will be used to help counsellors provide better support and guidance.

2. Data Privacy & Confidentiality

All data collected through this portal is encrypted and stored securely. Your individual responses will only be accessible to authorized university counsellors and psychologists. No identifiable data will be shared with third parties without your explicit permission.

3. Voluntary Participation

Your participation in this assessment is entirely voluntary. You have the right to skip any questions you are uncomfortable answering and you may withdraw from the assessment at any time without any negative consequences.

4. Right to Anonymity

While your UAI ID is used for system identification, reports generated for general university research are anonymized and do not link back to your personal identity.

5. Right to Withdraw

Even after giving consent, you have the right to request the deletion of your assessment records by contacting the psychological support cell.

By clicking "I Agree & Continue", you acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms above and voluntarily agree to participate.
